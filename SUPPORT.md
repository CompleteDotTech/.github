# Support

## Getting help

- **Bugs and questions about an original repository:** open an issue on that repository. Say what you ran, what you expected and what happened.
- **Behaviour of a fork:** if the problem is in upstream code, ask upstream. Issues about upstream behaviour will usually be closed here.
- **Security problems:** do not open an issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Anything else:** see [complete.tech](https://www.complete.tech) or [@CompleteTechLLC](https://x.com/CompleteTechLLC) on X.

## Expectations

Projects are published as-is and many are prototypes. Responses are best-effort, and there is no guaranteed turnaround or support for any specific repository.
